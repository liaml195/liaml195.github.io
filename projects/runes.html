Runes

Runes is an adventure/combat centered game in development in which the player traverses the world and recollects gemstones that can be used in order to amplify weapons, allowing unique effects to be applied based on the elements.
The player must retrieve all runes and combine them to create an ultimate weapon, which will aid in the battle against the main boss.
